title: "E2B Contributor License and Feedback Agreement"
---

Welcome to our Contributor License and Feedback Agreement! Here's a quick breakdown of what's going on:

- You (the contributor) would normally be entering into an agreement with E2B.
- We deeply appreciate your interest in contributing to our software.
- Right now, we're still getting everything set up on the legal side to properly accept and manage contributions.
- Because of that, we're not able to accept outside contributions at this time.
- Once we're ready, we'll update this agreement and open things up for contributions.

In short, we're excited that you want to contribute — we're just not quite ready yet.
